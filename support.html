Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea for the app? Email and you will get a reply from the developer.

Email ruben.reut@icloud.com

For bugs, include your iPhone model, iOS version, the level you were on, and what happened. A screenshot helps.

No sound?

The app plays through the media volume, so the ring/silent switch does not mute it. Turn the volume up with the side buttons while the app is open, and check that no headphones or Bluetooth speaker are connected and playing elsewhere.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone only. Unlocked levels, stars and settings are stored in the app's local storage and are never sent to a server or synced between devices. There is no account.

How do I start over?

Delete the app and install it again from the App Store. That removes all progress. There is no in-app reset yet.

Does the app collect any data?

No. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no third-party SDKs. Details are in the privacy policy.

What about the web demo?

The web demo is an older browser version of the trainer. It saves progress in your browser's local storage for this site; clearing site data for rubenreut.github.io removes it. On a computer you can tap with the Space bar. The App Store app is the current, complete version.

Something else?

Email ruben.reut@icloud.com. Questions about these pages are answered there too.
