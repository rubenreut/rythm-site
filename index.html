What you get

  • 76 progressive levelsEach level adds one idea: eighth notes, rests, dotted notes, syncopation, ties, triplets, 6/4 time.
  • Tap along, get scoredA metronome counts you in, then every tap is graded Perfect, Great, Good or Miss. Haptics confirm each hit.
  • Read the notationStandard rhythmic notation on screen, so your sight-reading improves with your timing.
  • Three-star ratingsEarn stars to unlock what comes next, or replay earlier levels for a full three-star run.
  • Free practice at any BPMSet your own tempo. Slow it down to learn a pattern, speed it up as you improve.
  • No account, no trackingProgress stays on your device. No subscriptions, no ads, no analytics.
